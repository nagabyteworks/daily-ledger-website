Daily Ledger Support

Need help with Daily Ledger? We're here for you. Please contact our support team regarding any of the following issues:

Email Support

support@nagabyte.com

We typically respond within 24-48 hours.

What we can help with:

  • Account Problems: Issues logging in or creating an account via Google Sign-In.
  • Backup Problems: Issues exporting or restoring local device backups.
  • Google Drive Backup: Troubleshooting Google Drive synchronization and restoration.
  • Notification Problems: Issues with scheduled reminders not firing.
  • Transaction Import: Problems importing CSV files.
  • Billing/Subscription: Questions about in-app purchases.
