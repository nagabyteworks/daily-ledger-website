What happens when you delete your account?

  • Profile Data: Your account profile, email address, and authentication data will be permanently deleted from our active databases.
  • Financial Data: All financial transactions stored on our servers will be deleted.
  • Data Retention: Some data may be retained temporarily in secure server backups or for legal/compliance reasons where strictly required by law.

What about Backups?

  • Local Backups: Account deletion does not automatically delete local backup files stored directly on your device storage. You must delete those files manually through your device's file manager.
  • Google Drive Backups: Account deletion does not delete data backed up to your personal Google Drive. You must delete those backups manually by managing your Google Drive storage.
