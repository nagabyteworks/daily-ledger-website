Delete Your Daily Ledger Account

Daily Ledger by Nagabyte allows you to permanently delete your account and associated data.

Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Daily Ledger account, enter the email address associated with your account and submit the deletion request.

Your request will be verified before deletion to prevent unauthorized account deletion.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, the following data associated with your account will be permanently deleted:

  • Daily Ledger account and profile information
  • Google Sign-In account association within Daily Ledger
  • Expenses and financial records stored in your Daily Ledger account
  • Custom categories and preferences
  • Cloud-synced or server-side data associated with your account
  • Other personal data associated with your Daily Ledger account

Data That May Be Retained

If any information must be retained to comply with legal, regulatory, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting obligations, that information will be retained only for the period required by the applicable obligation and securely deleted afterward.

Important

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Deleting your Daily Ledger account does not delete your Google Account. It only removes your Daily Ledger account and the data associated with it.

If you use Daily Ledger in Guest Mode, no server-side account may exist. Locally stored guest data can be removed by uninstalling the app or using the app's data-clearing option.

Need Help?

For questions regarding account deletion or your data, contact:

  • Developer: Nagabyte
  • App: Daily Ledger
